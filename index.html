01 / Experimental research

Same Score, Different Story

When social and monetary rewards lead to similar outcomes, are they really interchangeable?

Behavioral experimentsQuantitative analysisfMRI
Project snapshot

In a controlled study of 107 adults, social and monetary rewards produced comparable improvements in task performance relative to control conditions, while finer-grained analyses distinguished their neural activity patterns during reward anticipation. Higher Need to Belong was associated with a stronger social-relative-to-monetary neural response, without a significant association with task performance. This correlation does not establish a user segment. First-author publication in the Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience (2026).
