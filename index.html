01 / Experimental research

Same Score, Different Story

When social and monetary rewards lead to similar outcomes, are they really interchangeable?

Behavioral experimentsQuantitative analysisfMRI
Project snapshot

A controlled study of 107 adults found comparable behavioral effects of social and monetary rewards, alongside differences in finer-grained neural response patterns. First-author research published in 2026. A full case study page is coming next.
